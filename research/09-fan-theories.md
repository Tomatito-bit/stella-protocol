# 09 · Fan theories: "what NASA is hiding"

Basis for the game's **classified events** (`app/src/data/secretEvents.js`). Each one is presented as a mystery; if the player investigates, the real scientific explanation is revealed.

| Event | Theory | Real explanation |
|---|---|---|
| The Face on Mars | A face carved by a civilization in Cydonia (Viking 1 photo, 1976) | An eroded mesa; low sunlight and image glitches formed the "eyes". Mars Global Surveyor (1998, 2001) showed it is pareidolia. |
| A door in the rock | Entrance to a base on Mars (Curiosity, May 7 2022, "East Cliffs") | A natural fracture the size of a dog door: layered rock cracked and a block fell out. |
| The Phobos monolith | Artificial structure on Mars' moon (MGS, 1998) | A boulder about 85 m across, probably thrown out by an impact; its long shadow makes it look like a monolith. |
| Lights on the Moon | Alien bases on the Moon | Meteoroid impact flashes (NASA has monitored them since 2005). LRO has photographed the Apollo sites since 2009: landers, footprints and flags, no bases. |
| The Black Knight | A 13,000-year-old alien satellite (STS-88 photo, 1998) | A thermal blanket lost during a 1998 spacewalk: space junk. |
| The Wow! signal | An alien message (Big Ear, Ohio, Aug 15 1977) | It never repeated. Hypotheses: a hydrogen cloud lit up by a star (2024 study) or a comet. **Still not fully solved**. |

Other theories reviewed but not used: the "floating spoon" (Curiosity, 2015) and the "rat" (Rocknest) on Mars, both rocks and pareidolia; the supposed Apollo hoax, disproved by LRO photos.

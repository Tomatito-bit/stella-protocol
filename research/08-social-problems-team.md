# Social Problems on Board (team research, November 14, 2026)

They are implemented in `app/src/data/socialEvents.js`.

| Tier | Problem | Who | Game effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | Clashes over routines and shifts | Everyone | −5% morale if not addressed |
| Easy | Technical misunderstanding | Engineer and pilot | Extra power used that turn |
| Intermediate | Science versus safety | Specialist and captain | Backing the specialist gives more science but stresses the captain; backing the captain loses that turn's science |
| Intermediate | Spacecraft wear | Pilot and engineer | If you side with the pilot, the frustrated engineer repairs worse |
| Intermediate | Ignored fatigue | Doctor and captain | Without rest, health drops and human-error risk rises |
| Intermediate | Third-quarter syndrome | Everyone | −20% performance until a break or a call with Earth |
| Hard | Scientific disobedience | Specialist | She takes radiation and the doctor is busy treating her |
| Hard | Deadlock in an emergency | Pilot and engineer | The turn is lost with no defensive maneuvers |
| Hard | Authority crisis | Captain and doctor | Turns must be spent mediating to restore command |
| Hard | Panic in the cabin | Everyone | 2 turns of penalties to health, energy and science; only safe mode with rest stops it |

**When the hard ones appear:** only under stress, meaning low morale, a high radiation dose, few supplies, or right after a storm.
